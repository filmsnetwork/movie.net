Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what movies.net ("we", "the app") collects and how it is used. By using the app you agree to it.

What we collect

  • Account details if you sign up: your email address, name, country and birth year.
  • Your activity: your My List, Favorites, and how far you watched titles, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Usage information such as which titles are played and for how long, used to understand which content is popular and to fix problems.
  • On your device: settings, watch progress and a saved copy of your account name are stored in your browser or app storage.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To sync your list and watch progress across your devices.
  • To improve the app, fix errors and keep it secure.
  • To send account emails such as email confirmation and password reset.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

Your data is stored and processed by Supabase (database and sign-in) and the app is hosted by Cloudflare. Account emails are sent through an email delivery service. Titles may show information and images from public movie databases. Video is played from the sources the app links to, which have their own policies.

Advertising

The app may show ads. Ad partners may receive basic technical information such as your device type and approximate location, as described in their own policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Connections use HTTPS and access to data is limited by account permissions. No service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: moviesnetwork@proton.me
